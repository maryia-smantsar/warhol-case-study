# Case Study: The Andy Warhol Museum App

**Year:** 2026  
**Role:** UX/UI Designer  
**Contributions:** User Research, Wireframing, High-Fidelity UI, Usability Testing  

## 01. The Problem

Museum visitors often face friction when trying to access information or buy tickets. Through my research, I noticed that tourists, in particular, feel overwhelmed by cluttered websites and language barriers. I needed to solve this by creating a fast, intuitive experience focusing on three key user needs:

* **Speed:** Completing a ticket purchase in under 60 seconds.
* **Clarity:** Easy navigation through current and upcoming exhibitions.
* **Accessibility:** Multilingual support for international tourists.

## 02. The Approach & Design System

To truly highlight the artwork, I chose a strict minimalist visual identity. I used a high-contrast monochrome palette for the interface, allowing Warhol’s signature pop-art colors to stand out only on key elements.

> "I wanted to capture Warhol's playful aesthetic and bring it into the modern digital world without overwhelming the user."

For the typography, I selected Neue Montreal because it provides a clean, modern hierarchy that makes reading on mobile devices effortless.

## 03. Testing & Final Impact

I built a high-fidelity prototype and conducted moderated usability testing with different personas. While younger users navigated the app instantly, older tourists hesitated when looking for the ticket button. 

Based on this, I adjusted the visual hierarchy and moved the primary call-to-action to a more prominent location. The final product simplifies the visitor journey into a seamless digital companion.
